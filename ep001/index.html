EP001 · Forgotten Jobs · 1890 · Maine, USA

The Night Shift in the Lantern Room

Through one night, a keeper trims the kerosene lamp's wick, rewinds the weight-driven clockwork that turns the Fresnel lens, and records the night in the station's log.

What the film simplifies

The keeper is a composite, not a real person. Not every Maine light rotated in 1890, and winding intervals varied by station. We found no source for the year keepers' uniforms began or for brass wick scissors, so the film claims neither.
