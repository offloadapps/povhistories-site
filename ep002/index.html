The Laundry That Ran on Urine

In a Pompeii fullery, you tread wool in tubs of stale urine, then rinse, brush, bleach it over sulphur and press it. Most fullers here were enslaved.

What the film simplifies

The worker is a composite. Recipes varied: urine, nitrum, potash and fuller's earth were all used, and no step-by-step manual survives. The order of washing, rinsing, drying, brushing, sulphur, earth and pressing follows Smith and Pliny and is the standard scholarly reading, not a recorded routine.
