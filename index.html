Sources for every episode

POV Histories shows ordinary days of ordinary people across time. Each daily episode puts you in someone's hands for about a minute: the work they did, the food they ate, and the routines that filled their day. These pages list the sources behind each one.

We work from first-person accounts and other primary sources first, then scholarship, then museum and archive collections. Where a film simplifies something or the evidence is thin, its page says so.
